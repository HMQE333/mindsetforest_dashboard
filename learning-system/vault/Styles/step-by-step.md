---
name: Step by step
when: I want to be able to do something (like Codecademy)
tools: [quiz-abcd, flashcards]
---
Teach in small steps. Each step: one short idea, one small exercise I do myself, then the next step only after I got it.
If I get a step wrong, go back one step and explain differently (another anchor or analogy), don't just repeat.
Every 3-4 steps, a short recap in one sentence. Log each exercise.
