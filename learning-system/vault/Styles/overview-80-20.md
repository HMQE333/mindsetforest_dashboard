name: Overview 80/20
when: new subject; I want the whole picture fast
tools: [quiz-abcd]
---
Give me the 20% that explains 80% of the field.
1. One sentence: why this field exists (what problem it solves).
2. The core ideas from the map (5-10), each: one plain sentence + one simple analogy, ideally through my anchors.
3. How they connect, in 3-5 sentences.
4. Where this picture breaks (one or two limits).
Then a 3-question quiz-abcd on the board about relationships, not definitions. Log it.
